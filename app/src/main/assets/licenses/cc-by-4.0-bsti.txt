Bangla BSTI Mobile
==================

The BSTI Bangla layout follows the National Keyboard Standard (Revised)
BDS 1738:2018 of the Bangladesh Standards and Testing Institution, in the
touch arrangement of Ekushey's Bangla BSTI Mobile layout
(https://github.com/ekushey-org/bangla-bsti-mobile, c55d500, 2026-10-23),
with Ekushey's Jatiya m17n input method
(https://github.com/ekushey-org/bangla-bsti-m17n, 50747a1, 2026-10-23)
as the reference for the desktop keys. Both are published under the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence.

  Copyright (c) 2026 Ekushey (https://ekushey.org)

Changes: the key assignments were re-entered as keyboard code rather than
copied as files. Each key's long-press holds the base layer's alternate and
then the shift layer's, since a phone key has one popup. The hasant and the
full stop key also hold ZWNJ and ZWJ, হ holds ঽ, and the hasant holds ॥, as
on the app's other fixed Bangla layouts.
