Third-party code in the GiellaLT speller library
================================================

GiellaLT spellers run in a native library (libwmdivvun.so, built from
native/divvunspell-jni) on Divvun's divvunspell, released by UiT The Arctic
University of Norway and its contributors under MIT OR Apache-2.0, and the
Rust crates listed below. Each crate remains under its own licence; the SPDX
licence expression from its Cargo metadata is listed beside it. "A OR B"
means the crate is offered under either licence at the user's option; this
app receives every crate under the permissive option of its expression.

Two C libraries are compiled in through their -sys crates: Zstandard
(zstd-sys; BSD-3-Clause, offered with GPL-2.0-only as an alternative) and
liblzma from XZ Utils (lzma-sys; 0BSD and public domain). The licence texts
accompany each crate's source at https://crates.io/crates/<name>, and
divvunspell's at https://github.com/divvun/divvunspell. The Apache-2.0 text
is bundled in this app (apache-2.0.txt).

The spellers themselves are not part of the app. The phone downloads them
from GiellaLT's own releases, or the user imports them; each is under the
licence of the language repository it is built from (GPL-3.0 or LGPL-3.0
for the ones the app offers), with its source at
https://github.com/giellalt/lang-<code>.

This is the complete set of crates compiled into the Android library.
