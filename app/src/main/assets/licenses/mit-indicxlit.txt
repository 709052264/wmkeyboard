IndicXlit
=========

Neural suggestions on the Indic phonetic layouts run IndicXlit v1.0 (English
to Indic), the transliteration model AI4Bharat trained on the Aksharantar
corpus: https://github.com/AI4Bharat/IndicXlit. The keyboard downloads its
weights, exported to ONNX and quantised to int8 without other change, from the
wmkeyboard-data repository (scripts/export_indicxlit.py there). Licence:
