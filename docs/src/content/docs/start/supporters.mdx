WM Keyboard is free, with no ads and no paid tier. The people below sponsor it through [SupportKori](https://www.supportkori.com/wasimaster), the yellow button in the corner of this site, or with a tip in the Google Play version of the app. Thank you.

Every SupportKori sponsor is credited by name. You're listed on this page with whether you made a one-off payment or joined the monthly membership, how much it came to and the date, and your name also appears in the app under **About › Supporters**. The credit stays up for as long as the list does; it isn't cleared when a new version comes out. Sponsors can also request features: email [arianmollik323@gmail.com](mailto:arianmollik323@gmail.com) with the name you sponsored under and what you'd like to see.

The same names are in the app under **About › Supporters**. The app shows names only; what each person gave and their message are on this page.

## How the list works

A name appears here when someone sponsors on SupportKori, either with a one-off payment or by joining the monthly membership. Each entry shows the name you typed on SupportKori, whether it was one-off or a membership, how much it came to, and the date. SupportKori doesn't pass on the message you write there, so only messages left with a Google Play tip appear here. If you left the name blank or chose to stay anonymous, nothing is listed.

## Tipping from the Google Play version

Google Play doesn't allow apps to link to another payment site, so the Play version of the app can't open SupportKori. It has a tip jar of its own instead, under **About › Supporters**. You pick a tier, type the name you want listed and, if you like, a message, and pay through Google Play like any other purchase. Turn on **Stay anonymous** to tip without being listed at all. The tip unlocks nothing. The rest of the app is the same either way.

A Play tip is listed with its tier's price in US dollars, such as $4.99. Google Play charges a local price in each country, so what you paid in your own currency can differ a little from the figure shown.

After Google Play confirms the payment, the app sends the name and message you typed and the purchase receipt to this site. The site checks the receipt with Google before it adds the name, so no one can add a name without a real tip, and it never sees your card or Google account. Stay anonymous, or leave the name empty, and nothing is sent at all. If the app is offline at that moment, it keeps the name and tries again the next time you open Supporters.

The page shows the list as it was when the site was last built. **Check for new supporters** asks for anyone added since then; nothing is fetched until you press it. The app works the same way: it ships with a copy of the list and only goes online when you press its button.

Want your name or message taken off? Email [arianmollik323@gmail.com](mailto:arianmollik323@gmail.com) and it will be removed.
