WM Keyboard is free, with no ads and no paid tier. These people chipped in anyway, through [SupportKori](https://www.supportkori.com/wasimaster) or the yellow button in the corner of this site. Thank you.

The same list is in the app under **About › Supporters**.

## How the list works

A name appears here when someone sends a tip or joins the membership on SupportKori. Only the name you typed on SupportKori is shown, along with whether it was a tip or a membership and the date. Amounts are never published. If you left the name blank or chose to stay anonymous, nothing is listed.

The page shows the list as it was when the site was last built. **Check for new supporters** asks for anyone added since then; nothing is fetched until you press it. The app works the same way: it ships with a copy of the list and only goes online when you press its button.

Want your name taken off? Email [arianmollik323@gmail.com](mailto:arianmollik323@gmail.com) and it will be removed.
