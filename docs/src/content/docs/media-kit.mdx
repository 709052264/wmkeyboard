## Logos

Every logo comes as an SVG, which scales to any size, and a large transparent PNG. Pick the version made for your background: the dark ones have white type, the light ones navy.

<Logos />

### Using the logo

- **Do** give it room. Keep clear space on every side at least as tall as the "W" in the wordmark.
- **Do** use the one-colour mark when print, embroidery or a single-ink job can't reproduce the gradient.
- **Don't** recolour, stretch, rotate or outline it, or add shadows, glows or other effects.
- **Don't** set the name in another typeface or rebuild the icon. Use the files.
- **Don't** imply an endorsement. Using the logo to point at the app is welcome; using it to suggest WM Keyboard backs your product is not.

Write the name as **WM Keyboard**: two words, "WM" in capitals. Not "WMKeyboard", "Wm Keyboard" or "WM keyboard".

## Typography

The wordmark is set in [Inter](https://rsms.me/inter/), in a heavy display weight, and shipped as outlines so it looks the same with or without the font installed. Inter is also the face on the store graphics and banners. Inside the app, text follows the phone's system font.
