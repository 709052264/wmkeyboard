Settings now come in three depths, Simple, Standard and Everything, and the
far-fetched features sit behind Extensions you turn on as a whole. Suggestions
are ranked by a neural context model, glide learns from your own swipes and
gets a tuning of its own for German, and swipes can be set on any key across
every layout. New tools: Music ID, an internet speed test, a to-do list and the
keyboard's own share sheet. Dictation picks how it hears the end of a phrase
and can type romanized languages back in Latin letters.

### Settings

- Settings depth: Simple, Standard or Everything, with "more settings" folds
  on every page. Extensions switch whole features on or off, and Simple starts
  with none. The depth lives under **Settings › About**.
- Gestures, Clipboard and Corrections are split into pages, with pinned
  keyboard previews and an animated demo for each gesture.
- Settings that are new since your last update wear a gold border, and a bar
  steps from one to the next ([#970](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/970)).
- A setting that needs another one hides while that one is off; a greyed-out
  control tells you why on a long press.
- Favourite screens and settings open from a star in every top bar and from
  the keyboard test button ([#898](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/898)).
- Every settings row has an icon, and a screen reached a second way names the
  path you know it by ([#979](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/979)). A searched-for row stays in view ([#904](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/904)).
- The first-run wizard asks fewer questions.

### Suggestions and autocorrect

- A neural context model ranks suggestions, a learned gate decides when to
  autocorrect, and a later word can revise an earlier one. English comes in a
  standard and a large model; Banglish has one too.
- A word typed in full keeps its two nearest one-key slips on the strip, so
  "is" offers "us" ([#979](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/979)).
- The undo chip keeps a chain of the last five corrections.
- Snippets can expand the moment their trigger is typed.
- Canadian and Australian English word lists ([#975](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/975)).
- The personal dictionary can drop the words a word list already knows ([#981](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/981)),
  is sorted by language ([#930](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/930)), and a word added by hand can carry a shortcut
  ([#931](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/931)).
- Phonetic typing remembers the word you picked for a spelling, as desktop
  Avro does.
- A phonetic language picked under Also suggest from offers its readings on
  a Latin keyboard: typing `marathi` on English offers मराठी.

### Glide

- A glide check-up fits the decoder to your own swipes, and each language can
  have its own tuning, German first, with a guard on compound joins and more
  weight on the word before ([#966](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/966)).
- Compounds the list lacks are read from their parts, and words of up to 32
  letters glide.
- A stroke that strays onto the number row reads as the top row, and letters a
  key carries on Shift or a hold count in glide and typing ([#970](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/970)).
- The word one key off a glided word shows on the strip ([#979](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/979)).
- A glided word holds its space before a full stop or closing bracket, and
  spaces searches in a browser's address bar ([#974](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/974)).
- Words either side of a spacebar crossing read with open ends ([#968](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/968)); no
  second space after a word swiped over a highlighted one ([#955](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/955)); pronoun
  contractions lead where a clause starts ([#949](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/949)); you choose how many swiped
  words a full search reaches ([#905](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/905)).

### Keys and layouts

- Swipes on keys: set once in Settings, laid over every layout.
- Delete word and Delete word forward as key actions ([#980](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/980)), and a Toolbox key
  action for any key.
- Your own hold symbols on every layout; a layout's own key height, spacing and
  side padding; every key made to match the letter keys in one step.
- Bigger key hints, hint and label offsets, and a per-key hint size. A held
  flick can repeat.
- The spacebar can swipe up for the number pad or be left blank, drops its
  arrows when it has no room for them, and moves the cursor faster.
- Resize by dragging a row ([#912](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/912)), split gaps up to 60% ([#952](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/952)), and a key's
  width as a share of the row in the layout editor. Layouts in use export as
  one file.
- In landscape, the toolbar's tools can stand in a column beside the keys.
- Bangla: the BSTI (BDS 1738:2018) layout ([#901](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/901)), Avro and Khipro on the 3×3
  flick board, আ and এ after a vowel on the fixed layouts ([#900](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/900)), and newer
  Khipro specs without waiting for a release ([#909](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/909)). KASROZ and ClearFlow gain
  a language key ([#929](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/929)). Sorani types ٠-٩ ([#972](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/972)).
- Khipro has its own Type English words as English switch, off by default,
  instead of sharing Avro's, and keys typed inside a Bangla word stay Bangla
  with no space after a pick there ([#986](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/986)).

### Languages

- Chinese: switch Simplified and Traditional from the keyboard ([#962](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/962)), keep the
  reading on the strip instead of in the field, and choose what a language
  switch does with it ([#973](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/973)). Sogou .scel cell dictionaries import as word
  lists.
- Japanese: edit inside a reading, with punctuation joining it ([#960](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/960)), and
  glide on Kana JIS ([#958](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/958)).
- A Bengali–English offline dictionary.
- Burmese typed in drawn order lands in Unicode order; Turkish ı counts as an
  accent on i.

### Voice

- Phrase end detector for live dictation: Silero, FireRedVAD, loudness, a
  model you import, and Smart Turn to end a phrase when you sound finished
  ([#976](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/976)).
- Dictate a romanized language and get Latin letters back ([#948](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/948)).
- The microphone stays open between clips ([#957](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/957)), and clip engines can split
  and undo a phrase ([#921](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/921)).
- Asr4All, Parakeet, Moonshine, Sherpa and Omnilingual models import from files
  ([#954](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/954)).
- Spoken punctuation in Portuguese and Spanish; delete swipe and cursor drag on
  the voice keys ([#925](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/925)); the strip returns over the compact bar for English
  words ([#910](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/910)).

- Putting a tool box's keys down shows the panel under them ([#961](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/961)).
- The Apps tool shows folders and work-profile apps ([#919](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/919)), ([#924](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/924)). The
  floating button gains app slots, categories and shapes ([#922](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/922)), ([#923](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/923)),
  ([#926](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/926)), ([#933](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/933)), and Back closes its menu ([#938](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/938)).
- AI: deep research, chat modes and answer formats, and an optional
  continuation chip in the strip ([#944](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/944)).
- Search: SearXNG category chips ([#934](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/934)), the engines behind each result ([#932](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/932)),

- Clock: notices for snoozed, coming and missed alarms, world clocks in a grid

- A PIN tab in the password generator ([#899](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/899)). KDE Connect can pause clipboard
  sharing ([#918](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/918)) and says what the always-connected link can promise ([#936](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/936)).

  on the NPU ([#903](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/903)).

### Emoji, stickers and clipboard

- Arrange the emoji panel like Gboard, SwiftKey or HeliBoard. Emoji mixes, two
  emoji as one sticker. Emoji 18.0, and emoji added by hand.
- Pick which stickers of a pack to import, and delete several at once ([#965](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/965)).
- The GIF and sticker panels can open fresh ([#906](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/906)), and the search boxes have a
  clear button ([#907](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/907)).
- A clip that is a colour code shows the colour, and each clip wears the icon
  of the app it came from.

### Backup and privacy

- Frequent backup, a second schedule with its own sections; up to 100 backups,
  or older ones kept spread out in time.
- Pause backup and sync, sync only on Wi-Fi ([#928](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/928)), and keep this device's
  theme ([#935](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/935)). The passphrase key is derived with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA512.
- Incognito by app: always or never, whatever a field asks.
