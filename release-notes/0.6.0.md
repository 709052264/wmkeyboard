A Clock tool, Notes and Location tools, an AI reply drafter, frosted and
liquid glass themes, and offline dictation that no longer depends on Google's
runtime: Whisper runs on whisper.cpp, and Asr4All, Parakeet and Moonshine run
on ExecuTorch. The lite edition, and so the F-Droid build, now carries the
grammar checker and those dictation engines, compiled from source.

### Tools

- **Clock**: world clocks, alarms, timers, a stopwatch, a day planner, date
  arithmetic and a history of what rang.
- **Notes**, with tags, checklists, Markdown, a lock, a trash and backup
  ([#560](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/560)). Hold a word in a note to select, copy, insert or ask the AI about it.
- **Location**: share where you are or a saved place, as coordinates, a map
  link or a page anyone can open; read the places people send you and point
  the compass at them; stamp the place on a camera photo; an optional map
  behind the dial from your own tile server ([#350](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/350)).
- **AI reply** drafts an answer to what somebody sent you, from a screenshot
  or the text, with tones, a model picker and drafts that stream in. Gemma
  can read the screenshot on the phone, and what you have already typed
  becomes the brief ([#234](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/234)).
- **Find & replace** as a toolbar tool, with recent searches, preserved case
  and `\n` and `\t` in regex replacements ([#627](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/627)).
- The AI tool can call **MCP servers** ([#573](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/573)) and send extra request headers
  per provider ([#648](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/648)).
- The clipboard batch from [#612](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/612): copies added to the recent row by hand,
  combining clips, reordering, filters and copy rules, with parts of a fresh
  copy offered on the suggestion strip. Edited clips are marked ([#576](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/576)), a
  pinned clip asks before it goes ([#636](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/636)), and the clipboard over the keys has
  an ABC/space/enter row and its own height ([#615](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/615)).
- GIFs: favourites, an opt-in Recent chip and offline copies ([#597](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/597)); GIFs and
  stickers tapped in a row are queued. Telegram sticker and emoji sets import.
- Vocabulary shows meanings in your language ([#651](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/651)) and imports Anki decks
  and Quizlet or CSV flashcards.

### Voice

- **Offline Whisper runs on whisper.cpp**, with prompts and any of Whisper's
  99 languages ([#416](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/416), [#440](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/440)).
- **Offline Asr4All** decodes English as you speak ([#289](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/289)); **Offline Parakeet**
  covers 25 European languages and **Offline Moonshine** is a small English
  model, all three on one ExecuTorch runtime.
- **Cactus Whistle** as a third offline engine, in the full edition ([#548](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/548)).
- A FUTO-style microphone switch on every voice surface, a choice of
  microphone, a switch that turns voice typing off everywhere ([#578](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/578), [#581](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/581)),
  and an opt-in language chip on the compact bar ([#603](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/603)).
- The system recognizer: words spoken after a pause land after 3 seconds,
  not 12, a recognizer that never answers no longer leaves the spinner up
  ([#664](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/664)), and a stalled on-device model falls back to the network ([#581](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/581)).

### Typing and gestures

- **Two-thumb typing**, and letters typed then finished with a swipe ([#595](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/595),
  [#638](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/638)).
- **Two, three and four finger swipes** run an action ([#647](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/647)), **slide to a
  key** types it on the lift ([#658](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/658)), and a swipe up from the spacebar opens
  the 2-D touchpad ([#444](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/444)).
- **Swipe up on the strip for more words**, slide to pick one, or show them
  as a grid.
- Call or text a contact by typing their name ([#656](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/656)).
- Autocorrect stops re-correcting a word you keep deleting at one spot.
- Real Shift+Enter in document editors ([#214](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/214)); a lone "i" is capitalized and
  one-letter words are learned ([#620](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/620)); learn from whole texts, with glide
  seeded from shapes you have used ([#449](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/449), [#453](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/453)).
- Caret, delete, auto-space and selection fixes from [#613](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/613) and [#662](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/662).

### Layouts and languages

- **Import layouts** from Windows (.klc), macOS (.keylayout), Linux XKB,
  Unexpected Keyboard and CleverKeys, Multiling O, and .cin, Rime and m17n
  input methods. Hunspell dictionaries ([#619](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/619)), Gboard's personal dictionary,
  FUTO Keyboard themes and other keyboards' backups import too, the last
  from onboarding.
- **Korean 나랏글 (Naratgeul)** with add-stroke and double-consonant keys
  ([#599](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/599)), and the 한/영 key on hardware keyboards ([#621](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/621)).
- Chinese: T9 pinyin syllables in a column beside the keys ([#635](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/635)), whole
  readings ranked with word pairs ([#580](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/580)), slips, phrase learning and 联想,
  Traditional conversion word by word with OpenCC's phrase tables ([#609](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/609)),
  and imported word lists in the candidates ([#633](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/633)). Japanese and Chinese
  punctuation on every input method ([#610](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/610)).
- Khipro Dark as a second Khipro layout ([#631](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/631)); Arabic ؟ on every question
  key and the harakat on the period hold ([#605](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/605), [#606](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/606)).
- Hold actions anywhere in a key's popup ([#570](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/570)), flick arms shown or hidden
  per layer ([#588](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/588)), the bottom row reaching over the board's bottom padding
  ([#649](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/649)), one-handed and split set per screen shape ([#611](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/611)), and any language
  hidden from the switcher ([#618](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/618)).

### Look and feel

- **Frosted glass themes** and the Frost family, with colours from the
  wallpaper, and **liquid glass**, a lens style for glass surfaces.
- Themes can **match the colours of the app** being typed into ([#592](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/592)), and
  keys, the toolbar and the strip can be picked on the preview to style
  them ([#265](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/265)).
- Panels grow from the button that opened them, swipe sideways between tabs,
  and have a drag bar when stacked over the keys ([#615](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/615)).
- A floating keyboard button that summons the keyboard without a text field
  ([#374](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/374)), and the system keyboard bar hidden on Android 16 and newer ([#582](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/582)).
- Any tools beside the suggestions, at their own size ([#462](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/462)); hold a tool for
  its HeliBoard partner ([#639](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/639)).

### Privacy

- App lock answers to its own PIN, password or pattern.
- Per-app keyboards ship off and are offered during setup.
- Copy a web link to any setting from the row itself ([#646](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/646)).

### The lite edition and F-Droid

The lite edition now has the Harper grammar checker, Offline Whisper on
whisper.cpp, and Asr4All, Parakeet and Moonshine. F-Droid builds all three
engines from their sources with the app, and its build is still reproducible:
it ships with the same signature as the APKs below. Harper's library is down
from 5.5 MB to 2.4 MB, and faster.
