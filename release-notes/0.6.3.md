A redesigned AI chat with memory, a search panel with an Images tab, and many
more things that run on the phone: search by meaning, a sticker generator,
picture upscaling, document and QR scanning without Play services, three
on-device translation engines, and offline voices for 1,132 languages. Chinese
and Japanese gain RIME and Mozc as optional engines, prediction moved into
native code, and recent words get their own row.

### Re-cut (versionCode 38)

0.6.3 was re-cut as versionCode 38 on 23 October, with these on top. It
installs over the first build as an update.

- The app switches to your phone's language by itself on Android 11 and later,
  instead of staying in English until you found **Settings › About › App
  language**.
- The offline download is 22 MB instead of 275 MB. It no longer carries every
  interface language and feature library: fetch the ones you want on another
  device and import them, and each feature that needs a library says which
  file to get. **Offline, all languages** keeps all 48 languages built in, for
  Android 7 to 10 ([how](https://wmkeyboard.pages.dev/privacy/offline/)).
- Importing an interface language pack works; it was unpacked and refused.
- The AI chat's Reasoning switch works with every Claude model, which now each
  get the thinking setting they accept, and the default models are newer.
- A fix that only changes capitals, like "i" to "I", no longer takes down the
  undo chip of the real correction before it.
- The arrow at the end of a scrolling suggestion strip can be turned off
  ([#890](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/890)).

### AI

- The AI chat is rebuilt: one message box with a + menu for pictures, files
  and text, answers at full width, reasoning and tool calls folded into steps,
  a Reasoning switch, and chats that rename themselves ([#882](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/882)). Several chats
  can be picked and deleted at once.
- AI memory: chats can remember and forget things, kept on the phone and
  recalled by meaning in later runs.
- On-device models run through llama.cpp for any GGUF model, with GPU and
  picture support, and the F-Droid build gets on-device AI too.
- The Settings assistant can be turned off ([#830](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/830)), and its Stop, Clear and
  errors behave ([#819](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/819)), ([#828](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/828)), ([#873](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/873)), ([#877](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/877)). The chat and the assistant
  keep Data Saver's Cloud AI policy ([#826](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/826)), and a tool round runs only the
  tools it offered ([#825](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/825)).
- New AI tools read your snippets, clipboard, notes and photos, the last two
  by meaning as well as by words.

### Search and tools

- The search panel has an Images tab, Tavily's search depth, More results at
  the end, a menu on each result, and the engine's answer drawn like a chat
  answer with pictures under it ([#879](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/879)).
- Search by meaning, on the phone, across the AI tools and the gallery.
- A sticker generator, and ×2/×4 upscaling for stickers and photos.
- Text scan, QR and barcode reading and a document scanner work without Play
  services, and text can be read from a picture in the gallery ([#869](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/869)). Pages
  a chat app would not take open in its share screen ([#848](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/848)).
- Translation adds Bergamot and NLLB-200 as on-device engines beside ML Kit.
  LanguageTool joins Harper as a grammar provider.
- Quick note, with a row of the latest notes ([#864](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/864)).
- Each provider has its own settings screen, and providers show up in settings
  search.

### Voice

- Offline Sherpa and Offline Omnilingual (Meta's 1,600-language model), with
  hotwords, added punctuation and capitals, and Strong noise reduction.
- Offline Whisper can run on the GPU.
- Read back speaks text aloud, with offline voices for 1,132 languages.
- Transcription server clips go up as Ogg Opus, a fraction of the size.

### Typing and glide

- Recent words are their own row in Rows & bars, inline or steady, with
  alternatives on show and only this field's words ([#814](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/814)), ([#842](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/842)), ([#868](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/868)), ([#894](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/894)).
- A No space action, like Nintype's spaceless key ([#851](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/851)), ([#867](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/867)). The space bar
  and arrow keys take flick arms ([#863](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/863)), ([#865](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/865)).
- Fix several missed keys is now Off, Careful or Eager, and reads several wide
  taps in one word as slips.
- A scrolling word strip has an arrow to the rest of its words ([#890](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/890)); the space
  after a suggestion can be off for one language ([#813](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/813)).
- FUTO Swipe is an optional neural glide engine for English QWERTY, and
  Google's gesture library can be imported as another.
- Two-thumb typing: split the last swipe off a merged word ([#832](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/832)), and fixes
  to previews, pauses, Backspace and the all-up space ([#818](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/818)), ([#831](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/831)), ([#841](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/841)), ([#849](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/849)), ([#858](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/858)), ([#870](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/870)), ([#871](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/871)), ([#895](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/895)).
- Glide types the word on screen at the lift ([#816](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/816)), glides saved words with
  apostrophes ([#854](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/854)), reads short doubled words as the common word ([#886](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/886)), and
  judges hint and capital flicks at the lift ([#859](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/859)), ([#896](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/896)).
- Prediction and glide decoding now run in native code.
- Full-screen typing in landscape can be turned off ([#883](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/883)), and the floating
  keyboard has a Leave field action and keyboard-up tap bindings ([#626](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/626)).

### Languages

- Chinese: librime (RIME) as an optional engine with a T9 syllable column and
  联想, and Pinyin letters carry digits and symbols on long-press ([#888](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/888)).
- Japanese: Google's Mozc as an optional engine. Chinese and Japanese
  handwriting work without ML Kit.
- Bangla and Hindi phonetic typing can let IndicXlit pick the space-bar word,
  and Khipro is listed as Khipro Classic ([#892](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/892)).
- Burmese: Zawgyi is detected, converted and can be typed.
- Thai, Lao, Khmer and Burmese runs are split into words for prediction.
- Spell checking: imported Hunspell dictionaries, Voikko for Finnish, and
  GiellaLT spellers.
- A layout can carry its own name on the keyboard.
