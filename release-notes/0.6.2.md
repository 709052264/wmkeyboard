Look up any word from any app, a Settings assistant that explains and opens
settings, files sent from the keyboard, and a smart home tool for Home
Assistant, Google Home, SmartThings, Hue, openHAB, Smart Life and SwitchBot.
Two-thumb typing gets a long list of fixes, 128 languages gain word lists from
Tatoeba, and the F-Droid build is one APK per processor instead of one APK
carrying all three.

### Tools

- **Look up** a word from any app's selection menu, Yomichan-style, with
  Yomichan and Yomitan dictionaries imported, offline English dictionaries,
  and a word's translations into your other languages ([#765](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/765)), ([#665](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/665)), ([#764](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/764)).
- **Settings assistant**: ask it about a setting and it explains, opens or
  proposes the change ([#788](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/788)). The settings root has an AI row, and Panels can
  be split into its own page ([#759](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/759)).
- **Send files** as encrypted links or to a phone nearby ([#729](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/729)).
- **Smart home**: Home Assistant with live tiles, control cards and an Assist
  bar, and Google Home, SmartThings (more than one account), Hue, openHAB,
  Smart Life (Tuya) and SwitchBot beside it, as one list of homes.
- **Calendar** events can be added, edited and deleted inside the keyboard.
- **Text repeat**, and a chip that offers it for text typed three times.
- The Todo plugin has due dates and reminders ([#722](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/722)); the clock writes every
  Discord timestamp style ([#770](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/770)); the scanner reads a code from a saved
  picture; snippets take a column count ([#471](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/471)).
- Translate, web search, photos, GIFs and stickers, dictionaries and currency
  rates are ordered provider lists with fallback. New ones: Mozhi,
  SimplyTranslate, Lingva and Translite ([#726](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/726)), Rens ([#727](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/727)), and GifCities.
- Text recognition reads Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Devanagari, with
  PaddleOCR outside Play ([#757](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/757)).

### Clipboard

- Clips as QR codes, tags, and pinned clips in folders by tag ([#766](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/766)), ([#768](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/768)).
- Link cleaning removes more trackers, and Undo brings the copied link back ([#762](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/762)).
- Clipboard search and Recent copies are actions for a key, flick or hold.

### Typing, glide and two-thumb typing

- Two-thumb typing: per-thumb hint and capital flicks ([#746](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/746)), the space
  after all thumbs up taken back, split pauses and a pause band ([#747](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/747)), and
  swipes that start on a flick key, double a letter, or end on a tap
  ([#736](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/736)), ([#737](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/737)), ([#738](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/738)), ([#740](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/740)), ([#741](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/741)), ([#742](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/742)), ([#743](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/743)), ([#744](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/744)), ([#782](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/782)).
- A recent-word bar fixes a word after you have typed past it ([#787](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/787)); hold a
  strip word and slide to rank it up or block it ([#790](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/790)); Retype word takes the
  last word back out ([#791](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/791)).
- Lists continue on Enter in any app ([#694](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/694)); autocorrect can be off for one
  language ([#802](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/802)); a mode can turn glide and two-thumb typing off ([#753](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/753)).
- ?123 chords run actions ([#754](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/754)), one floating button has gestures and a
  quick menu ([#755](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/755)), and the selection row can stay up with nothing selected ([#763](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/763)).
- Statistics count voice typing and two-thumb words, with streaks, bests and a
  live speed on the spacebar ([#751](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/751)), ([#752](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/752)), ([#761](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/761)), ([#796](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/796)).
- Voice typing reopens where the keyboard left it ([#699](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/699)), and dictation can
  be corrected while it runs, with Asr4All typing as it hears ([#758](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/758)).

### Layouts and languages

- Word lists and word pairs for 128 languages from Tatoeba, 40 more
  dictionaries, and Bosnian QWERTZ with Bosnia suggesting Bosnian.
- Bangla: Mayabi, Samsung and Shörolipi layouts ([#803](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/803)), and glide on Khipro
  Dark with the keys shown as pressed ([#772](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/772)).
- Gboard+ English and Hebrew, each with a swipe-safe twin ([#794](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/794)).
- The layout editor draws the real board, edits several keys at once ([#769](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/769)),
  puts text flick arms on Enter and Shift ([#789](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/789)), and binds a key to a saved
  mode ([#795](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/795)).
- Japanese Romaji has digit and symbol hints ([#731](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/731)).

### F-Droid

- One APK per processor: the arm64 download is about 13 MB smaller than one
  APK holding all three ([#801](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/801)). Version codes now end in a digit for the
  processor, and the GitHub APKs carry the same digits, so GitHub and F-Droid
  installs of one version still go over each other.

  glide share keys again ([#720](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/720)); I'll and I'd hold their places on the strip

- Web editors keep the space ahead of the caret ([#760](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/760)); start and end of text
  move the caret in every app ([#785](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/785)); Shift+Down moves a line in Firefox ([#800](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/800)).
- The toolbar comes back after a fingerprint unlock ([#793](https://github.com/wasi-master/wmkeyboard/issues/793)); Back closes a
